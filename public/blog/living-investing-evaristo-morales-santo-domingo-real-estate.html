Living and Investing in Evaristo Morales: Santo Domingo Luxury Real Estate Spotlight

Santo Domingo continues to stand out as one of the Caribbean's most dynamic capital cities, blending historic charm, vibrant cosmopolitan culture, and rapid economic expansion. Among the capital's premier residential sectors, Evaristo Morales has earned a distinguished reputation as a prime destination for luxury living and strategic property investment.

Whether you are an international investor seeking resilient rental yields or a luxury buyer looking for an upscale Caribbean residence, Evaristo Morales delivers an unbeatable combination of prime location, high livability, and long-term capital appreciation.

Why Evaristo Morales is a Premier Residential Address

Situated in the heart of Santo Domingo's National District (Distrito Nacional), Evaristo Morales is bounded by major transit and commercial arteries including Avenida Winston Churchill, Avenida 27 de Febrero, and Avenida Gustavo Mejía Ricart. This central positioning places residents within minutes of the city's top financial hubs, upscale shopping centers, fine dining, and international schools.

1. Unmatched Urban Connectivity

Living in Evaristo Morales allows effortless access to the city's most prominent lifestyle and business destinations:

  • World-Class Retail: Moments from BlueMall Santo Domingo, Acrópolis Center, and Downtown Center, offering luxury retail brands, cinema complexes, and premium gyms.
  • Gastronomy and Social Life: Walking distance or short drives to the renowned culinary corridors of Piantini and Bella Vista.
  • Top Medical and Financial Facilities: Proximity to premier private clinics, hospitals, corporate headquarters, and international banking institutions.

2. A Serene Yet Connected Neighborhood Feel

Unlike purely commercial sectors, Evaristo Morales preserves tree-lined residential streets, tranquil boutique cafes, and a community-oriented ambiance while keeping the vibrancy of Santo Domingo right at your doorstep.

Essential Purchasing Considerations for Foreign Buyers

Buying real estate in the Dominican Republic is straightforward and legally secure for foreign nationals.

  1. Equal Property Rights: Foreign individuals and entities enjoy the exact same constitutional rights to purchase, hold, and sell real estate as Dominican citizens, with no special foreign ownership quotas.
  2. Title Verification and Due Diligence: Working with experienced local real estate advisors and legal counsel ensures clean title deeds (Certificado de Título), verification of property taxes (IPI), and seamless closing.
  3. Tax Incentives: Certain qualified developments benefit from the CONFOTUR Law (Law 158-01), which grants substantial tax exemptions, including a waiver of the 3% property transfer tax and annual property taxes (IPI) for up to 15 years. Read more on CONFOTUR tax savings.
